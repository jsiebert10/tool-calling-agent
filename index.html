Cadence

A running coach for people with a schedule. Tell it your week and it finds the driest, safest times to run, builds the route from your door, and makes a playlist that hits every footstep on the beat.

What it can do
🗓
Plan your weekFits runs around class and work, dodging rain, storms, heat and smoke.
🏃
Today's run"I want to do my run today": the workout, paces, and a fresh forecast.
🗺
Routes from your doorReal loops to parks and tracks. Flat ones for intervals, big ones for long runs.
🎧
Stride-matched musicElectronic tracks whose BPM matches your steps per minute.
